## Current delivery and donation rules

- Program portal applications appear in both Programs and Volunteers (including the separate admin pages), with pending requests first, applicant email/message/profile review and Approve/Reject actions. Lists use the organization-scoped application endpoint without requiring the separate programs fetch to finish first. Approval/rejection sends an in-app notification. Approval creates an invitation; the volunteer reviews the brief and confirms attendance before Assign Task becomes available. Assign Task on an approved application fixes the program and preselects that applicant. Assigned tasks appear in Volunteer Progress; volunteers start work and submit completion proof for organization review. New tasks notify the volunteer, and task status updates notify the organization.
- The program messaging query parameter is handled only on communication/dashboard pages; donation links use their program parameter solely for selecting the donation recipient.
- Closing Messages & Ratings consumes the notification's program query parameter while preserving other query parameters and the active tab. Admin tab changes and reloads keep the popup closed; its explicit button and new notification links still open it.
- Overlapping delivery/replacement/resource-collection assignments are allowed. Direct-allocation candidates display conflicts; volunteers see the overlapping jobs in their review and must explicitly acknowledge the conflict. Both assignments remain scheduled. The warning is recorded in job history and manager notifications. Program scheduling outside these delivery workflows retains its existing rules.
- Buyers can use Received on an order or replacement once it is out for delivery. Only the buyer can acknowledge it; the timestamp is recorded once and the seller/volunteer are notified. Buyer receipt does not approve delivery proof or change reserved stock.
- Availability & current location and Volunteer Progress use consistent card padding, compact headings, normal-size controls, a weekday checkbox grid and responsive program/task cards. Embedded program participation no longer repeats a second panel heading.

- Shop delivery: seller posts to the volunteer portal using a saved or job-specific pickup address. The buyer address comes from checkout and cannot be changed in the posting form. Organization/platform admin coordination is not required. The first eligible volunteer to accept owns the job and reports pickup, transit, progress notes and completion proof to the seller.
- Replacement: buyer submits a claim for a delivered shop order; seller approves and reserves stock, then posts the replacement job to the portal. The first eligible volunteer accepts, collects the buyer return, exchanges it at the seller and delivers the replacement. The seller can enter an exchange address or use the saved address; buyer address is automatic. Progress reports go to the seller.
- Order cancellation: available only during the first hour after purchase and before pickup. The API enforces the limit; cancellation restores stock/accounting once. A replacement claim remains a separate post-delivery workflow.
- Resource donation: donor chooses the recipient organization and related program. The responsible org admin allocates a particular active volunteer whose availability toggle is on and current location is saved. There is no portal allocation option or purchase return/replacement offer for donations. Previously posted, unclaimed portal collections are hidden and require direct admin reallocation. Pickup address/date/time come from the donor; reports and proof go to the org admin, whose confirmed usable receipt updates stock and issues the donor certificate.

The entries below record earlier workflow revisions; these current rules take precedence.

# Implemented workflow changes

Follow-up fixes (November 1, 2026):

- Resource donations require explicitly choosing an active organization. Both donation forms use live recipient options, show published needs, filter available active programs to that organization, and clear the program when the organization changes. Program selection remains optional for organization-wide donations. The API validates organization identity and program ownership and never infers the recipient from the donor's account or a program alone.
- Organization admins publish item names, quantities and units through **Publish Resource Needs** in Resource Inventory; this does not require editing bank details. Donors see the needs in the donation recipient selector and organization cards.
- Both donation forms store structured item names, quantities, units and conditions. A collection has one accepted volunteer; duplicate offers and competing acceptance are blocked. Collections default to a direct volunteer invitation.
- Confirming usable warehouse receipt creates one immutable resource thank-you certificate with donor, organization, program, received item quantities, units, date and certificate number. An in-app notification links the donor to the certificate, and donation history offers **View Thank-You Certificate** with **Print / Save PDF**. Certificates use confirmed quantities rather than assuming all offered items were received.

- Program cards have **Messages & Ratings** for admins; accepted participants have **Program Messages & Rating** in their programs. Program group and direct messages have sender/recipient visibility checks, authenticated account access, notification links, and five-second updates while the dialog is open. Completion ratings are whole numbers from 1–5, require verified completion of every assigned work item, retain written feedback, and update the volunteer's average without counting edits as additional ratings.
- Assign Task from a program keeps that program fixed in the task form. Global task assignment still allows selecting a program. Volunteer candidates offer View volunteer details without selecting the volunteer.
- Delisting removes programs from discovery and blocks new attendance, work and applications. Member history shows the current paused status; open application dialogs disable submission. Program views poll every five seconds and refresh across browser tabs after changes. Participants receive program status notifications.
- All submitted phone numbers must contain exactly ten digits beginning with 6, 7, 8 or 9; country prefixes and spaces are rejected. Shared frontend validation and a global API body validator cover contact fields, including nested payloads. Email validation accepts valid addresses from all providers. Optional empty contact values remain optional.
- Application approvals and rejections notify the applicant and link to their own actor dashboard, including beneficiary accounts. Approved community applicants receive a volunteer directory record if one is needed for their invitation; they still review and accept the work brief separately.

- Program shift weekdays use independent checkboxes so multiple days can be selected directly.
- Active programs open for applications appear in the volunteer portal through `/programs/portal`, independently of public Programs listing approval. Application submission uses the same eligibility rule; invite-only and paused programs stay out of the portal.
- Closing program Details safely clears the selected record without crashing the admin page.
- Participation and handovers appear in program management. Purchase and replacement panels appear in shop, resource management and actor dashboards instead of every page.
- Checkout shows available stock below quantity immediately, flags excessive quantities, and validates against refreshed stock before payment.
- Sign-in always opens the authenticated account's role dashboard, including when an older redirect destination exists.
- Portal cards and application forms offer an Organization & Program Details dialog with organization contacts and impact, program work, dates, weekdays, reporting time, meeting point, coordinator and required skills. Opening it from an application preserves the draft message.
- Admin volunteer Details now combines account information with volunteer skills, contact details, service history, location, availability and travel preferences. Applicant Details also shows the application message, date, status and program. Reviewed applications remain visible; only pending applications expose approval actions. Applicant profile access is limited to the owning organization or platform admin, and profile responses use an explicit field list excluding credentials and account recovery data.
- Volunteers can save their own biography, home location, date of birth, name, phone and skills from Edit Profile. Age is derived from date of birth. Planned availability and travel settings are available on both Edit Profile and the volunteer dashboard, with weekday checkboxes. Personal updates cannot change roles, account approval or verification; verification remains a registration-controlled status. Approved volunteer accounts without a separate directory record receive one when saving their profile or availability.

The changes implement the selected follow-up requests from the workflow audit. The broader audit remains a separate backlog.

- **Volunteer selection:** program and delivery planners show current availability, location freshness, planned dates/weekdays, service areas and travel willingness. The server checks accepted program/delivery commitments and blocks overlapping shifts. “Available now” is informational; future attendance must be confirmed separately. Location is self-reported, with matching against entered service areas; this is not GPS verification or a distance calculation.
- **Programs:** selectable start/end dates, shift days and times, reporting time, response deadline, meeting point, coordinator and work brief. Direct invitations and approved applications lead to a full organization/program review before acceptance. Changes to material details create a new version and require reconfirmation. New task allocation requires confirmed attendance.
- **Withdrawal:** pending applications can be withdrawn. Members can withdraw before work starts; after starting, the organizer reviews pending proof and transfers unfinished work to another accepted member or cancels the remainder with a note. Completed work, partial-progress notes and approved hours are retained. Reading application lists no longer reactivates memberships.
- **Work and certificates:** verified completed subtasks are locked and do not block progress on unfinished work. Program and participant completion use verified tasks and subtasks together. A stored certificate identifier is issued only when all assigned work is verified complete for an active, confirmed member.
- **Resource fulfillment:** allocations must match the requested item name and unit. Delivered and reserved quantities are tracked separately; partial deliveries leave the outstanding quantity open for subsequent batches. Delivery issues move through open, investigating, resolved and recipient-confirmed closed states, with reopening and history.
- **Purchases:** eligible signed-in actors have purchase history and replacement entry points. Before pickup (or after a confirmed return), cancelling an order restores stock and reverses the recorded demo sale exactly once. Cancelling a delivery remains distinct from cancelling the purchase. Hidden products are archived so existing purchases can still be reconciled.
- **Replacements:** review must approve and reserve stock before a scheduled invitation. Volunteers accept/decline, can withdraw before pickup, and can report failure. Coordinators confirm return of all goods before retrying or cancelling after pickup. Seller/organization reviewers verify completion evidence. Cancellation releases reserved replacement stock.

Schedules currently use **Asia/Kolkata** and same-day shift windows. Payments and reversals remain demonstrations; no bank transactions or refunds are performed. Existing records without full scheduling details must be completed/rescheduled before a new commitment can be accepted.

Validation includes real Nest-service integration scenarios, browser workflow regressions, frontend unit tests, TypeScript builds and frontend lint. Browser tests use controlled API fixtures; backend integration tests exercise actual services and their shared state.


### Automatic shop delivery addresses
- Sellers save a full pickup address in Seller Dashboard using Save Pickup Address. A city or shop name alone is no longer used as a delivery address.
- Arrange Delivery and Reassign Delivery fetch and display the seller's saved pickup address and the buyer's exact address from checkout as read-only fields. Delivery cannot be arranged until the seller saves a pickup address.
- The backend uses these canonical addresses even if a submitted pickup field differs, scopes address access to the responsible organization/seller, and refreshes the addresses when reassigning. An existing delivery retains its address until reassignment.


### Delivery coordination: organization admin, seller and volunteer portal
- Organization admins choose a delivery date, time window, both route areas and an exact collection point for donated resources. They can post to the volunteer portal or invite a particular active volunteer who has switched Available now on and saved a current location. Planned dates, weekdays, service areas, travel preference and overlapping accepted work are checked again on acceptance.
- Sellers cannot browse delivery volunteers or directly invite them, including through replacement assignment endpoints. Sellers can post their shop order or approved replacement pickup to the volunteer portal, or send an order-specific/replacement-specific request to their organization admin using Request Volunteer from Org Admin.
- The volunteer portal now loads real shop deliveries, donor collections, beneficiary resource deliveries and replacement pickups. Apply & Accept explicitly accepts the work immediately; the first eligible applicant owns the job. It is not a pending application requiring a second approval. Portal applicants consent to the proposed shift, even when their Available now toggle is off, while planned availability, travel preferences and scheduling conflicts are still checked.
- Portal acceptance notifies the seller (for shop/replacement work) and every approved admin of the responsible organization. Direct invitation acceptance, declines and coordination changes also notify the responsible managers. Private delivery addresses and buyer details are masked until acceptance.
- Shop flow: seller saves pickup address -> buyer checks out with delivery address -> seller posts to portal or requests admin help -> volunteer applies and accepts (or admin invites an available volunteer) -> pickup -> in transit -> completion image -> seller verifies proof -> order delivered.
- Donated-resource flow: donor selects recipient organization/program and enters items -> admin arranges collection -> one volunteer accepts -> collection proof verified -> admin confirms usable receipt -> inventory and donor certificate created. Beneficiary request -> admin reserves compatible inventory -> admin posts delivery or invites an available volunteer -> pickup -> transit -> proof verified by admin -> stock distributed and beneficiary notified.
- Replacement flow: buyer submits claim and issue image after a delivered order -> seller/admin approves and reserves replacement stock -> seller posts pickup to portal or requests admin help; admin may directly invite an available volunteer -> one volunteer accepts -> collect buyer return -> exchange at seller and deliver replacement -> completion proof -> seller/admin verifies -> replacement completed. Failures require confirmed goods return before retrying; cancellation before pickup restores reserved stock once.
- Legacy assignment actions now open the scheduled workflow. Reassignment retains schedule and collection addresses while reloading canonical shop addresses. Admin order and replacement lists are scoped to their organization.
- Validation includes real Nest HTTP route tests for role restrictions, portal address masking, notification recipients, single-volunteer claims, conflict handling, resource distribution and proof-verified shop/replacement completion, plus browser checks for the corresponding controls and mobile portal acceptance.


### Shop delivery section visibility
- Shop delivery operations appear for the responsible seller, organization admin and volunteers handling delivery work. Admin shop delivery controls are confined to overview/resources/shop areas.
- Donors and beneficiaries see shop delivery status through My Purchases instead of duplicate operational delivery cards. Their resource collection/delivery workflows remain available.
- Empty Shop Orders Awaiting Delivery sections are hidden for organization admins without pending shop orders.
- A volunteer buying an item sees it in My Purchases; delivery work appears only when assigned or offered to that volunteer. Seller delivery cards are limited to that seller, even when the seller is also a buyer.


### Resource donation allocation without duplicate scheduling
- Resource donation collections use the donor's saved pickup address, date and time slot. Organization admins choose only a volunteer or Post to volunteer portal; they do not enter pickup time or place again. Reallocation also reloads donor details.
- Morning slots map to 09:00?12:00, afternoon to 12:00?16:00 and evening to 16:00?19:00 India time. Availability and conflict checks use that donor-selected window. Expired donor slots cannot be assigned.
- The receiving organization and registered city are shown for handover coordination. Volunteers can use Report Collection to record quantities, condition or handover details; the report is stored in history and notifies the responsible admin. Pickup confirmation, image proof verification and usable receipt confirmation remain required before inventory/certificate issuance.
- Shop orders, replacement pickup and beneficiary resource delivery retain their separate delivery scheduling forms.


### Volunteer delivery review and acceptance
- View Full Details and Apply & Accept now open the same review dialog on the portal and volunteer dashboard. The dialog shows exact pickup/destination addresses, seller/organization, pickup window in India time, item quantities, work instructions and replacement exchange/return route before acceptance. Public portal cards continue to mask exact addresses.
- A current delivery was being rejected because the volunteer's saved available weekdays did not include its pickup date. The review explains saved preference mismatches. Explicit one-job attendance/travel confirmation allows a portal volunteer to accept that particular shift without changing saved preferences. Active-account requirements, schedule conflicts, expired offers and first-volunteer ownership remain enforced; direct admin invitations retain their availability checks.
- Missing directory profiles for already approved volunteer accounts are created when they review/accept; inactive or unapproved accounts remain blocked. A failed source assignment releases its provisional scheduling reservation. Repeated acceptance by the same assigned volunteer is idempotent, and changed posting details require a fresh review.
- Errors appear inside the review dialog with Reload Latest Details; success identifies the assignment and links to My Deliveries for progress reports and completion proof.
